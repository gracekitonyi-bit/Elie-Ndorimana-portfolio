About Me

I am Elie NDORIMANA, a Master's student at the African Institute for Mathematical Sciences (AIMS) Rwanda, specializing in Data Science. I am passionate about Applied Mathematics, Research, and Data-driven Policy.

With a Bachelor's degree in Applied Mathematics from the University of Rwanda, I have served as a Rwanda Olympiad Coach, Mastercard Foundation Scholar, and Former Head of Students in the Department of Mathematics.

My mission is to apply mathematical modelling and data science to solve real-world problems in health, environment, and education.
