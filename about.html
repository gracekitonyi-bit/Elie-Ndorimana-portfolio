Who is Elie?

I’m Elie Ndorimana, a passionate mathematician and aspiring data scientist currently pursuing a Master’s Degree in Data Science at the African Institute for Mathematical Sciences (AIMS) Rwanda under the Mastercard Foundation Scholarship. With a strong foundation in Applied Mathematics from the University of Rwanda, I’m dedicated to solving real-world problems through mathematical modeling, data-driven research, and technology.

Beyond academics, I serve as a Rwanda Mathematics Olympiad Coach and a part-time contributor at Totum Tuus Ltd. My interests span across data science, AI, research, and education, and I’m driven by the desire to use analytics and innovation to impact policy and empower communities.
