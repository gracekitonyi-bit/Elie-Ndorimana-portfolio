About Me

Elie NDORIMANA

I am a Master's student at the African Institute for Mathematical Sciences (AIMS) Rwanda, specializing in Data Science. I am passionate about Applied Mathematics, Research, and Data-driven Policy.

With a Bachelor's degree in Applied Mathematics from the University of Rwanda, I have served as a Rwanda Olympiad Coach, Mastercard Foundation Scholar, and Former Head of Students in the Department of Mathematics.

My mission is to apply mathematical modeling and data science to solve real-world problems in health, environment, and education. I am eager to collaborate on impactful projects and create innovative solutions.
